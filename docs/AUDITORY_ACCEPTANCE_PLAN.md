# Opt-in natural-audio acceptance and benchmark plan

No real anime, game, meme or musical-performance media was submitted by this
implementation task. Source files and raw evidence remain external to Git.
Each evaluation needs separate authorization for its bounded uploads, model,
privacy and budget. Existing readings, responses and failed probes are immutable.

Prepare source-first references before exposing evaluators to model answers.
Use separate reference preparers and output reviewers, retain disagreements,
include positive/negative controls and genuinely ambiguous abstention cases, and
reserve held-out passages. Twenty or more held-out examples across adequate
controls are required to qualify a task for a declared media category; a single
successful episode or owner review establishes no global reliability.

Keep contextual Beat It-family credit in MID/context evidence. Do not insert it
into the blind prompt or rewrite the earlier blind conclusion. Compare composition
and phrase correspondence independently of unresolved exact recording, arrangement,
performer or voice identity. A waveform nonmatch does not erase independent work
identity. Declare the audience-exaggeration claim's reviewed visual/text route with
audio supplementary. Declare any beat/cut synchronization separately, with adequate
temporal visual review, qualified audible-event timing and clock alignment required.

Produce a new packet, reconciliation and claim delta explaining whether the new
audio changes mechanism/scope, contradicts a premise or leaves the established
text/image reading intact. A valid experimental answer alone does not close a claim.

## Witch from Mercury E20 dialogue

Use the selected episode's actual subtitles and original dialogue track, excluding
commentary. Prepare short bounded intervals containing the disputed quoted word,
louder delivery and prolonged final syllable. Stage 1 excludes the canonical wording,
speaker name, expected emotion and prior reading. Independent references distinguish
lexical accuracy, vocal delivery and timing rather than scoring them as one impression.

Preserve AO-101 (reported Stop), AO-102 (louder), AO-103 (prolonged) and AO-104
(command reading) as separate items. The stronger source text can contradict AO-101;
only materially declared word-dependent interactional/emotional readings recheck.
AO-102/AO-103 need their own task and observation assessments. Stage 2 gets the
source-established text/discrepancy and the same bound audio, with explicit context
IDs and unchanged Stage-1 hash. Its agreement is contextual consistency, not an
independent transcript confirmation. Compare the final event reading in a delta.

## Recommended first real-media experiment

Begin with one authorized bounded Jensen interval to test EXPERIMENTAL collection,
atomic attribution and a preserved text/image route. Then use E20's disputed-word
passage for local correction and contextual-dependence checks. These experiments
inform benchmark design; neither can qualify the backend globally.
