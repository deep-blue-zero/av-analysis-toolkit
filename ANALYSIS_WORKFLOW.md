# Analyze from claims to evidence

The current targeted unit is one bounded dramatic event. Use `scene prepare`
and `scene plan`, or attach `--scene-packet` to `deep-read`. Read what dialogue,
vocal/musical performance, sound, movement, editing and composition jointly
construct. Preserve their separate evidence IDs beneath integrated prose.
Use `scene reconcile` to record exact proposition competence and explicit
adjudication, then `scene delta` to compare the result against a frozen earlier
packet. A lexical error reopens its dependents; it does not erase unrelated
delivery observations. Stage 2 contextual audio does not independently confirm
Stage 1. Generating frames is not a declaration that they were reviewed.
See [the event contracts and examples](docs/HOLISTIC_CROSS_MODAL_ANALYSIS.md).

1. Read the human's scope and method. Do an independent bounded source reading
   before consulting prior verdicts. Record available modalities and perception
   through `preflight`; select absolute streams and hash-bound sources.
2. Preserve canonical Japanese text. Use subtitle normalization only as a new
   derivative with an exclusion ledger. Subtitle times are navigation unless
   phonetic boundaries have been verified separately.
3. Identify a small number of claims whose adjudication could change the reading.
   Declare source locators, modalities, alternatives and uncertainty. Use
   `evidence-plan` to route the smallest consequential question.
4. Prepare retained contours once. Query bounded intervals with `contour-query`
   and compare within-character conditions with `compare`. Mixed soundtrack
   intensity and F0 do not isolate a speaker or prove an emotion.
5. Where motion matters, prepare a continuous `motion-window` and inspect it
   through a real video interface. Five-position frames are navigation. Cut
   candidates still need review; a still does not establish a transition.
6. Preserve native-rate, channel-aware bounded `audio-witness` clips. Use actual
   direct perception, a validated observer adapter, or human review only where
   auditory differences bear on the claim. Start with context-minimized questions.
7. Keep lyric/melody/composition recurrence separate from recording reuse.
   Reuse nonmatches do not prove a new take. Count performances once when building
   sufficiently supported, reviewed, within-speaker baselines.
8. Write atomic evidence reports with observations, measurements, auditory
   reports, inference, interpretation, counterevidence and alternatives visibly
   separated. SUPPORT requires recorded adjudication; keep unresolved claims OPEN.
9. For benchmarks, export neutral analyst context and separate A/B/C/D packets.
   Keep crosswalks and prior verdicts outside the reasoning inputs. Each packet
   needs an actual reading; quality gains require source-grounded adjudication.
10. Verify runs, preserve source-clock conversions, record cache lifecycle and
    package compact durable evidence. Use explicit hash-bound relocation mappings
    when moving to another host. Do not rewrite historical provenance.

`deep-read` prepares a preflight, plan, optional bounded local measurements and
an analyst handoff. It does not invent the initial reading, claim reasoning,
motion perception, auditory review or final literary analysis. Those stages
belong to the reasoning agent and whichever perceptual systems actually work.


Explicit `--method` always takes precedence, including in isolated/blind mode. Episode isolation constrains semantic evidence independently. See docs/METHOD_PRECEDENCE_PATCH.md and the current workflow; the embedded method is a fallback only.
